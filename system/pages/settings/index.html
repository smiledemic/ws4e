📄 Paging 🧭 Page behaviour 💬 Quotes 🃏 Bundles

📄Paging

How the library is laid out. Paged keeps the sheet's pages and you flip them with the arrows or ← / →. Pageless puts every asset on one long page. Click a card anywhere to use it; click Paged · A–Z again to flip between across and within pages. Any open WannaSmile tab switches over straight away.

🧭Page behaviour

Fine-tuning for paged layouts. Click a card anywhere to flip it between its two options. Local keeps you on the current page and only shows what matches there, so you can flip pages to see where each match lives. Global gathers matches from every page into one view. Pageless has no pages, so the paging options are switched off while it's on; the data cache still works.

💬Quotes

How the quote bar under the header behaves. The Qtype button next to it switches between scrolling left, scrolling right and a still quote that stays centred. Click a card anywhere to flip it. Any open WannaSmile tab switches over straight away.

🃏Bundles

Bundles are one card holding several versions of an asset (beta, v2, copies). Keep version assets hidden leaves the versions out entirely. The other versions fan out behind the front card like a hand of cards: click one peeking out, or Shift+click the card, to bring the next one up. Click a card anywhere below to flip it. Saved straight away and any open WannaSmile tab switches over.
