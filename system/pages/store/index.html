🫧Widgets

Picked widgets show up as floating bubbles on the main site. Got more than one? Press + / - to cycle between them. The one you leave minimizes instead of closing, and comes right back when you cycle to it again.

🎨Themes

Pick up to 9 themes as shortcuts, then press 1–9 on the main site to jump straight to one (0 / R always goes back to Redux). Activate makes a theme the one that loads next time on this device, and dresses this page in it right now.

🌀Gif packs

Gif packs are the loading, searching and crash animations. They're separate from themes, so mix and match freely. Add as many as you like to the cycle and press [ / ] on the main site to swap between them. Activate puts one in use right now. Hover a card to see it search.

🛠️ Your gif packs

Make your own: drop a gif for loading, loaded, ded, crash and search. They're saved only in this browser, and work like any other pack (activate them, add them to the [ / ] cycle).

🖼️Frames

Frames sit on top of your profile picture, in the header and on your profile card. Equip one to wear it right away, or press it again to take it off. You can also pick or upload frames from the picture menu on the main site.

🪟Wrappers

A wrapper is the background of your profile card (the banner stays on top of it). Equip one to use it right away, or press it again to take it off. Set how see-through it is with the Wrapper opacity slider in your profile's gear menu.

🖱️Cursors

Custom cursors swap your mouse pointer for a hand-drawn set, everywhere on WannaSmile. They're off until you turn them on. Activate a pack to switch it on and use it right away. Text boxes keep the normal typing cursor.
