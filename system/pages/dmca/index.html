DMCA Policy

How copyright takedown requests are handled.

©️Copyright on WannaSmile4Evr

WannaSmile4Evr hosts and links to third-party web content. If you believe something here infringes your copyright, you can ask for it to be removed under the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA).

A valid notice has to meet the three requirements below. They follow the notice-and-takedown standard in 17 U.S.C. §512, the same rule every service provider (site host, search engine, ISP) works under.

📋The 3 rules of a DMCA notice

1

Identify the copyrighted work

Name the specific work you own the rights to: the title, its assets, or the content you say was used without permission. Vague claims ("something on this site") can't be acted on.

2

Identify the material and where it is

Give the exact page or asset URL on this site where the material appears, so it can be found and reviewed.

3

Contact details and a good-faith statement

Include your name and a way to reach you, plus a statement that you believe in good faith the use is unauthorized and that your information is accurate, under penalty of perjury.

📨File a takedown request

Filing opens a new issue on the site's GitHub repository, pre-filled with the DMCA template. Fill in the asset, the work it infringes, and your contact details there.
